# Ecological Information Reference Knowledge Base

**EIRKB — Work in Progress — Academic Research Resource**

**Author:** Jonathan Minchin  
**Organisation:** Ecological Interaction  
**ORCID:** [0000-0001-8152-1796](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-8152-1796)

EIRKB is an evolving reference resource within Ecological Information. It
brings together research on the history and theory of ecological information,
intellectual lineages, conceptual classifications and academic references.
Classifications remain provisional and references are undergoing verification.

## Read the research

- [History and Theory of Ecological Information](history-and-theory.md):
  the historical arc, overlapping intellectual lineages and conceptual map.
- [Reference Register](reference-register.md): references, concepts, people,
  classifications, relationships and source notes.

The research is maintained independently in EIRKB. This local preview reads
the selected documents from commit
`bbc7c0e755a2504a450272691aed6741af5aa6a7`; it does not follow later research
commits or uncommitted edits.

The register's source-workbook link leads to that same version in the private
research repository and requires repository access. The workbook itself is
not included in this preview.

This is a local review interface. Public publication and hosted integration
have not been enabled. No publication date, DOI or licence is assigned here.
