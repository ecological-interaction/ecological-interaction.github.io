# Ecological Interaction

Ecological Interaction develops open research, computational methods,
and digital infrastructure for understanding and working with
ecological systems.

Our work is organised into six interconnected research layers.

## Research Layers

1. **Ecological Understanding** — Education, research methods and courseware.
2. **Ecological Information** — References, knowledge bases and datasets.
3. **Ecological Computation** — Tools for processing ecological information.
4. **Ecological Intelligence** — Indicators, models and decision support.
5. **Ecological Infrastructure** — Distributed ecological research systems.
6. **Ecological Generation** — Design, experimentation and interventions.

## Projects

Each research project maintains its own GitHub repository,
documentation and development history.

This website provides a central index to those resources.
